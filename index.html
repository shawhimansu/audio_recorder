How to use (Chrome or Edge):
1. Open the video in another tab.
2. Pick Audio only or Video + audio, and set a duration if you want it to stop by itself.
3. Click Start recording. In the sharing window choose the Chrome Tab with the video and keep "Also share tab audio" switched on.
4. Play the video (full screen inside the tab is fine). The green bar shows sound is coming through.
5. Click Stop (or "Stop sharing"), then download.

Video recordings are held in memory until you download them, so for very long sessions use Small or Good quality and download soon after stopping. The video file is MP4 when your browser supports it, otherwise WebM (VLC plays both). Firefox and Safari are not supported.
